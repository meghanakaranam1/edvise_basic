What to check:
• Switch to Teacher — "Knowledge base" and "Pending approval" disappear from the sidebar entirely, not just greyed out. "All documents" stays, since that's a general view, not an admin tool.
• Switch to Admin — both reappear.
• Click All documents (works in both roles) — it now shows real uploaded files (gradebook CSVs, PDFs) with filename, type, which conversation they came from, and a working download link — not the old mislabeled duplicate of "Action plans."
• This mockup also shows the defense-in-depth from the real code: even if a teacher somehow lands on the Knowledge Base view directly (not just via the hidden nav link), they'd see an access-denied style message, not the real admin tool.
• Click My actions — there's now just one sidebar entry instead of three ("Action plans" / "Meeting Agendas" / "Reports" used to each be their own sub-tab). Inside, everything shows together in one chronological list, each item tagged with a small colored badge (Plan / Agenda / Report) so you can still tell them apart — and filter pills at the top let you narrow to just one type without needing a separate nav item for it.
