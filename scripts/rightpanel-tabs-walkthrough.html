Right panel — tabs during note selection

Live reproduction of the Notes & Artifacts panel (RightPanel.tsx). Click a "Generate" button below to enter note-picking mode and see what happens to the tab row.

Requirement — hide the other tabs while picking notes
What to check:
• Normally all 4 tabs show: Notes · Action plan · Agenda · Reports.
• Click Generate Action Plan on a note (or any of the three generate buttons) — the tab row collapses down to just Notes while you're mid-selection. You can't accidentally click "Agenda" or "Reports" and wander off before finishing.
• Click the × on the picker, or Create to confirm — the full 4-tab row comes back.
